# Add change around key bindings and related

- STATUS: OPEN
- PRIORITY: 50
- TAGS: Modernization,Feature

Right now, some keybindings are a little off, such as CTRL-S being Search instead of that being CTRL-F, and other well known keybindings that aren't available, such as CTRL-Backspace, CTRL-Delete, Delete key (Delete == Backspace currently), Home (Brings cursor to begining of file, not beginning of line), End (Brings cursor to end of file, not end of the line), and also add support for the Scroll wheel as well, since it currently does nothing.
